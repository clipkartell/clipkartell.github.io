Clipkartell

Clipkartell posts short, vertical highlight clips from GTA 6 live streams on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

“Clipkartell Publisher” is the operator's private tool: it prepares clips, and the operator publishes them to his own accounts. For TikTok, every post goes through a “Post to TikTok” screen where the operator sees the connected account, edits the title, chooses the visibility and interaction settings, discloses commercial content and confirms before anything is uploaded. It is not a public app; nobody else can sign in.

Contact: clipkartell@proton.me

Clipkartell uses the YouTube API Services, the Instagram Graph API and the TikTok Content Posting API.
