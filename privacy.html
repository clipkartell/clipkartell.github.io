Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24.11.2026

1. What this app is

“Clipkartell Publisher” (the “App”) is a private tool used only by its operator to publish short gaming clips to the operator's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok and to read the performance statistics of those accounts. The App is not offered to other users; nobody else can sign in.

2. Data we process

  • Account authorisation: access tokens that the platforms issue when the operator connects his own account (OAuth). Tokens are stored only locally on the operator's computer.
  • TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API): the account's open ID, display name/nickname and avatar (shown on the “Post to TikTok” screen so the operator sees which account he posts to), the account's posting settings (allowed privacy options, comment/duet/stitch settings, maximum video length), and the status of each post. Scopes: user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload.
  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads to the operator's channel, video IDs, titles and channel/video statistics.
  • Instagram (Instagram Graph API): publishing Reels to the operator's professional account, media IDs, permalinks and insights (views, likes, comments, shares, saves).
  • Video files, titles and statistics are stored locally on the operator's computer. Published videos are public content chosen by the operator.

No data of other users is collected. No data is sold or shared with third parties. Nothing is used for advertising.

3. Platform terms

YouTube: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy. TikTok: TikTok Terms of Service, TikTok Privacy Policy. Instagram/Meta: Instagram Terms of Use, Meta Privacy Policy.

4. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time: Google – Google account permissions; TikTok – TikTok app › Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions; Instagram/Meta – Settings › Apps and websites. After revocation, locally stored tokens and data are deleted within 30 days, or immediately on request.
